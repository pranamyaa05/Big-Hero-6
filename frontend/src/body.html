Hard to use shouldn’t mean shut out.

Everyday objects shut out millions of people: they were never made for every hand.

  • One small part is in the way

    Most of these products work fine. What stops someone is one small part: a knob, a clip, a cap.

  • Built for one kind of body

    Jar lids, taps, keys, pens and canes assume strong grip, steady hands, good sight and easy balance. For people with arthritis, tremor, low vision or reduced mobility, they become barriers.

  • Two bad options

    Buy a whole new product or a specialist aid, or hope someone online already designed the exact part you need. Neither is fast, cheap or reliable.

How can generative AI turn an inaccessible object into a personalized, 3D-printable aid instead of forcing people to buy something new?

How it works

ReForm3D is an AI system that looks at an object you already own and generates a small, custom 3D-printable intervention that makes it accessible to the person using it, instead of generating new products from scratch.
